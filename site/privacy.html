Privacy policy: event registration

Who is responsible

CHYBIK + KRISTOF s.r.o., Dominikanske namesti 656/2, 602 00 Brno, Czech Republic, is responsible for your personal data when you register for an event in the programme of its exhibition at Aedes, Berlin (22 October – 3 December 2026). Contact: berlin@chybik-kristof.com.

What we collect and why

When you register for an event, we collect:

  • your name and email address (required), to manage the guest list and to tell you about changes or cancellations;
  • your company, phone number and a note (optional), to know who is attending, to reach you at short notice, and to take your message into account.

Legal basis: handling your registration for the event (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The optional details, and the technical data described below, are covered by our legitimate interest in organising the event (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Registering is voluntary, but without a name and email address we cannot reserve your place. Please don’t put sensitive information, such as health details, in the note.

The registration form is provided by Tally. When you open it, Tally’s servers receive technical data such as your IP address and browser details, which they need to show you the form.

Who receives your data

  • Tally BV (Belgium) provides the form and stores submissions in the EU on our behalf, under a data processing agreement. Tally sends us each submission by email through its provider SendGrid (USA).
  • Google Workspace (Google Ireland Limited) hosts our mailbox, where these emails arrive. Google may process data in the USA.

Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the EU’s standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data, share it with anyone else, or use it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We delete registrations, including the notification emails, within three months of the exhibition closing, by 3 March 2027.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict its use, or for a copy in a portable format, and you can object to its use (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Email berlin@chybik-kristof.com.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority: in the Czech Republic, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů; in Berlin, the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit; or the authority where you live or work.

We make no automated decisions about you.
